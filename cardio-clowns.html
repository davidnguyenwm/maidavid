About Cardio Clowns

Cardio Clowns is a fun and engaging cardio fitness app designed to make your workouts enjoyable. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey or looking to add some excitement to your routine, Cardio Clowns brings playful energy to every session.

Built with simplicity in mind, the app helps you stay active and motivated without the complexity of traditional fitness trackers.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 8, 2026

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. Cardio Clowns is designed with a privacy-first approach. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short: Cardio Clowns does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device.

Data Collection

Cardio Clowns does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected (name, email, location, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or tracking is performed
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • No cookies or similar tracking technologies are used
  • No account creation is required

Data Storage

All app data (such as preferences or progress) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is removed.

Third-Party Services

Cardio Clowns does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Since Cardio Clowns does not collect any data, it is safe for users of all ages. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Since the app does not collect data, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. You are in full control of any information stored locally on your device.
