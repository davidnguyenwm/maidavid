About

Senior software engineer with 10 years in industry plus a Ph.D. in mobile systems. At CARIAD (Volkswagen Group) I led the in-car App Store self-updates that reached ~60,000 production vehicles, and came up with the AI-personalised App Store that set the store's future direction. Today I work on the Android Automotive OS platform: the C++ Vehicle HAL and vehicle power management.

Before that, 6.5 years at Meta as Android tech lead for Facebook Stories and Video, then two Bay Area startups. I build my own apps too: CardioClowns and StoreBench.

I work across the stack, from Kotlin UI to C++ and the kernel, and I'm the person teams bring the hard bugs to.
